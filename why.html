Why Bidoof?

Bidoof is the cornerstone of many adventures. Incredibly reliable with its ability to learn a wide variety of necessary moves, many trainers couldn't get by without it. Maybe it wouldn't be treated as the powerhouse of the squad, but it is certainly the glue of the squad. Those people don't realize who the TRUE MVP by the end is.

Most importantly though, Bidoof is lovable. Its goofy smile and adorable stature make it a pokemon that is easy to love. Bidoof is described as the "plump mouse" pokemon, a perfect description. In total, you have:

  • Round body
  • Huge front teeth
  • Tiny eyes
  • Perpetually vacant expression

How could you NOT fall in love when you see such a goofy face yet a pokedex description that reads Bidoof has an unsophisticated face and is rarely flustered by anything. There have been incidents involving Bidoof sauntering into villages and gnawing on the houses without a single care. It's just a little guy who likes chewing. Nothing more, nothing less. Perfection
